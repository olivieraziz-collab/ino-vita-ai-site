Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Gestion Ino-Vita inc. is responsible for the personal information processed through this site. Person responsible for the protection of personal information: Olivier Aziz.

To contact him: info@ino-vita.com

Your visit

This site has no forms, accounts, cookies, audience measurement tools or advertising tools. Like any website, the server hosting it records technical connection data (IP address, date and requested page) to ensure its operation and security. These technical logs are kept by the site’s hosting provider and may be processed outside Québec.

  • The site uses a local script for navigation and animations. It stores nothing in your browser: no local storage and no saved preferences.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site itself: no request to any third-party service and no cookie set by a third party.

Your messages

If you write to us, we use your name, contact information and the content of your message to respond to you and follow up on the exchange. We do not sell this information or disclose it for advertising purposes.

If you book a call, scheduling goes through Cal.com, which may process your contact details outside Québec.

Your rights

You may request access to information about you and its correction by writing to the person responsible.

We respond in writing, diligently, no later than 30 days after receiving your request. If no response is provided within that period, the request is deemed refused. In the event of a refusal, you may request an examination of a disagreement by the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec within 30 days of the refusal or the expiry of the response period.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date.
