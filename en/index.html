The system

The system is built to stop, not to send.

01

Every review matters

Before an email goes out, every required review must be complete. The tool that drafts it never reviews its own text. If a review is missing, sending is blocked.

02

Limited access

The AI tools can access approved websites only. Every other connection is refused and recorded, even if a tool requests it.

03

Source documents prevail

A fact that does not match its source document blocks the email. Even a high review score cannot allow it through.

The functions to use for a client are defined after reviewing their needs. No client result is claimed until it has been measured.

Connections to financial accounts are read-only.
